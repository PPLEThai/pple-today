Test sends (ทดสอบส่งหาฉัน) no longer appear in other admins' แจ้งเตือน history, and their details and answers can't be opened by other admins. The admin who sent a test sees it, labelled ทดสอบ (เห็นเฉพาะคุณ 1 ชั่วโมง), for one hour after sending, and after that it leaves their history too.
