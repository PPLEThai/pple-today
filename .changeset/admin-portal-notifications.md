Admins can send notifications from the new แจ้งเตือน section, below Mini App. A notification can go to everyone, to some roles, to some provinces or districts, to one phone number, or to a list of numbers. It can carry an image, a link (a page in the app, a mini app, or an external URL), an option to skip the notification centre, and an SMS fallback for phone numbers. It can also carry a reply form with fields, buttons, an expiry, tray buttons and an iOS Live Activity. These notifications are always sent as PPLE Today. Answers to the form are kept in the portal: the notification's page lists them with who answered, counts the answers for each button, exports them to CSV, and can close the form early.
