On iOS 26, the tab bar shows the selected tab in an orange glass pill (hold and drag to switch tabs), and screen headers show a Liquid Glass bar when the page scrolls under them. The แอปฯ, กิจกรรม and ฉัน tabs show a compact title once their large title scrolls away, and the role picker on แอปฯ moves into the header. A user's profile shows their name in the header once it scrolls away. Alert dialog titles use the app's heading font.

On Android and older iOS, the tab bar's tabs are evenly spaced and centred again, and the selected tab is underlined.
